# 名画资料与中文化状态 / Painting metadata

已为原用英文的354件核查中文备注：16件补入有出处的作品中文名，200件补入作者中文名；其余338件作品名继续保留馆藏英文，不补暂译。原名保持不变，已核实备注现已排入对应200张屏保画面，详见[中文备注清单](TAB5-PAINTING-CHINESE-NOTES.md)。

For the 354 English-label works, source-backed notes now add 16 Chinese artwork titles and Chinese author names for 200 works. The other 338 titles retain museum English without provisional translation. Original labels are retained and verified notes are now rendered in 200 frames; see [Chinese reference notes](TAB5-PAINTING-CHINESE-NOTES.md).

当前402件均保留作品名、作者标注和馆藏博物馆。48件的作品名和作者已有中文（12件西方名画、36件中国画），另外354件仍为馆方英文原文。博物馆独立字段统一使用中文，画面署名保留原文馆名；200张带核实中文备注的画面已重新排版，部署情况见[.125验收记录](TAB5-ACCEPTANCE-125.md)。

All 402 paintings retain a title, author attribution and holding museum. Both title and author are Chinese for 48 works (12 Western paintings and 36 Chinese paintings); the remaining 354 retain museum English. Museum metadata is Chinese, while existing on-frame institution credits remain unchanged. Not redeployed.
