# TAB5 角落入口与天气明细

本地候选 `0.2.151-ui`，只调整 TAB5 界面，不修改协议、桥接或小屏固件。候选准备与原生预览不代表已经安装或发布。

两个右侧入口共用尺寸与定位：点击区域 56×56，右边距 30，圆角 16；设置按钮在顶栏居中，Codex 直达在底部导航行居中。图标按视觉重量居中，设置保留现有底色，Codex 保留透明背景和原品牌颜色。Codex 小图由原项目已登记的品牌资源预生成，避免运行时缩放；其他品牌资源字节不变。

天气页顶部在同一行显示城市／区县与来源。城市使用 30 px 浅白色 `#DFEBEE`，来源使用 24 px 灰蓝色 `#8EA5AF`，中间保留间距。按原始文本计算宽度，长位置名省略以保护右侧三个页签；缓存提示仍保留。「预报时间为当地时间」和温度卡片重复的来源／实况文字已移除，最低／最高温度重新布局。

原生预览与既有回归覆盖常规页面入口、Codex 会话／语音、天气预报滚动、完整／分块绘制、缓存发布和页面滑动。截图使用演示数据，保存在 `artifacts/development/tab5-ui-consistency-20261008/`。固件沿固定候选目录与 sidecar 工作流交付；此次未操作设备，也未修改其轮播设置。

## English

Local candidate `0.2.151-ui` changes only the TAB5 UI, leaving the protocol, bridge and ESP8266 firmware unchanged. Candidate preparation and native previews do not mean installation or publication.

Settings and Codex Direct share 56×56 touch areas, a 30 px right margin and a 16 px radius, centered within their respective header/footer rows. Their existing filled/transparent backgrounds and brand colors remain distinct. The small Codex image is generated from the existing attributed brand asset instead of scaled at runtime; all other brand assets remain byte-identical.

Weather location and source share one line: 30 px pale location text (`#DFEBEE`) and 24 px blue-gray source text (`#8EA5AF`), with spacing and a reserved tab area. Raw text determines widths, long location names truncate, and cached-data indicators remain. The local-time caption and duplicate source/current-weather line are removed; low/high temperatures are repositioned.

Native previews and existing regressions cover normal-page entries, Codex sessions/voice, forecast scrolling, full/partial rendering, cache publication and swipes. Screenshots use synthetic data under the development directory. Firmware follows the fixed candidate/sidecar workflow. No device operation, display-cycle change or public release is performed.
