# 每日书法图库 / Daily calligraphy catalog

2026-10-06：**411件独立作品、1500个展示帧**。先补入上次会话的63件（582张原图、602帧），达到366件年度数量；再按本次要求新增**45件不超过3页的作品**（56张原图、60帧）。此前303件及其画面保留，解缙180°方向修正保留。

There are **411 distinct works and 1500 frames**. The pending 63-work batch completes the 366-work annual target. Another **45 complete works, each with at most three frames**, add 56 originals and 60 frames. Prior works and the Xie Jin orientation correction remain intact.

本次短幅来自明尼阿波利斯美术馆26件、大都会17件、ColBase／东京国立博物馆2件。37件单页、1件两页、7件三页；其中8副对联、1组四屏各计一件，按右到左并排展示两屏，不删正文或落款。不明作者沿用馆藏“佚名”；尚未核实汉字的作者保留馆方罗马字拼写，不凭读音猜字。

The short batch contains 26 Mia, 17 Met and 2 ColBase works: 37 one-frame, one two-frame and seven three-frame works. Eight complete couplets and one four-panel set each count once. Paired layouts preserve every panel. Museum unidentified attributions and unverified romanized names are retained.

原图短边至少1000、长边至少2400像素；展示帧1280×720。来源、许可、裁切、排版和SHA-256逐图留档；新增602+60帧均经联系表检查。先前63件中34件为完整拓本，保留残损并区分碑刻和拓印年代。[分页统计](TAB5-CALLIGRAPHY-PAGINATION.md)与[本地候选库](TAB5-CALLIGRAPHY-CANDIDATES.md)分开记录。年度数量及本地功能校验通过不等于真机验收，本轮未部署或刷机。

Originals meet a 1000-pixel short edge and 2400-pixel long edge. All 662 added frames were reviewed. Sources, licenses, crops, layout and hashes are recorded. The previous batch includes 34 complete rubbings, with original damage and separate inscription/impression dates preserved. Local validation does not establish hardware acceptance; not deployed or flashed.

馆藏名称已逐件整理，当前清单按展示页数升序排列；画面底部保留馆方署名（部分为原文馆名）。馆藏不等于当前正在展出。
