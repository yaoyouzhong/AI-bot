# 书法短幅与本地候选 / Short works and local reserves

本轮新增45件3页以内作品，另保存8件完整候选（7件长条幅、1副低对比度对联），不加入每日catalog、不计入新增45件。所有长卷检索记录和已下载原图保留；只有局部照片、缺页、缺联或下载受阻的记录列为待核查，不能当作完整候选交付。

45 short works are admitted; eight complete reserves (seven long vertical works and one low-contrast couplet) remain local, outside the daily catalog and the 45-work count. Downloaded scroll sources are retained. Partial/incomplete/blocked material remains research evidence, not a complete delivered work.

本地目录：`artifacts/development/tab5-daily-art/calligraphy-candidates/`。`selection.json`及`render/provenance.json`记录来源和原图SHA-256；原图在同级`../originals/`，按路径引用而不复制大文件。`download-review-inventory.json`保留其他已下载资料及尺寸。`preview.html`可在浏览器查看新短幅与候选。
