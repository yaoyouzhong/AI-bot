# 中国画入选与备选 / Chinese painting selection

2026-10-06：36 幅加入每日名画，另外 24 幅作为备选；备选不计入轮换和年度作品数。原图均来自 Cleveland Museum of Art CC0 馆藏，每张已实际下载、解码并检查完整构图，短边至少 1200 像素、长边 3400 像素。作品名及作者使用馆方中文原文，保留繁体及异体字。

36 paintings are included in the daily catalog, evenly interleaved with 366 existing Western paintings (402 total). The 24 reserves are excluded from the active catalog. All selected sources are documented CMA CC0 images, checked at print resolution. Chinese captions retain the museum's original script.
