Source: Purple Mountain Observatory (PMO), Chinese Academy of Sciences.

Official annual-calendar index: https://pmo.cas.cn/xwdt2019/kpdt2019/202203/t20220309_6386774.html

AI-bot independently reads public solar-term date facts from the official annual calendar documents. It validates the target year, publisher, twelve months and 24 named solar terms, then writes its own normalized date cache. Source website content, PDF documents, their layout, images and third-party implementation code are not distributed with AI-bot. The source documents retain their publisher's rights; this attribution does not assert an open-source license for them or endorsement of AI-bot.

The index was checked on 2026-10-24 and lists annual documents through 2026. Later years not present in the index retain existing verified offline data and are not claimed as successfully downloaded from PMO.
